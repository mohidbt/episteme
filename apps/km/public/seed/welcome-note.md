# Welcome to Episteme 👋

You're poking around as a guest. Everything here is real — your edits save, your AI runs, your account row exists. Sign up whenever to keep it across devices.

## Things to try

- Type `/` anywhere to summon the slash menu (headings, lists, code blocks, callouts).
- Type `[[` to wiki-link to another note, paper, or reference.
- Highlight text → bubble menu → ask AI to rewrite, summarize, explain.
- Drag the handle on the left of any block to reorder.
- Click the paper in the sidebar to read a real PDF (RAG, Lewis et al. 2020).

## Why this exists

Two pillars: **tool consolidation** (no more Obsidian ↔ Scholar ↔ Zotero ↔ ChatGPT shuffle) and **agentic workflows** (Deep Agents with first-class access to every tool).

## Math

Wrap LaTeX in dollars and it renders: Euler's identity $e^{i\pi} + 1 = 0$ sits inline, while

$$\int_0^\infty e^{-x^2} dx = \frac{\sqrt{\pi}}{2}$$

gets its own line. Click into either one to edit the source; it re-renders when you click away.
